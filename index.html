01. About

Domain expert → AI engineer

I work in Indian civil aviation — and when I couldn't find an AI tool that understood the regulatory documents I dealt with daily, I built one. That side project became a production-grade RAG system with hybrid retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, and structured citation enforcement — no LangChain, no wrappers, just custom Python pipelines.


I'm a Mechanical Engineering grad from BIT Mesra (batch topper, CGPA 8.32) who self-taught the entire ML/AI stack: from PyTorch and sentence-transformers to MLflow, DVC, and Docker. Now I build LLM-powered infrastructure and apply MLOps discipline to every system I ship.
